The whole app is four tabs

Report the day, see the week, follow the time — and invite the team.

Built for low friction

Time tracking is something you have to do, not something you want to do. Every decision in the app shortens the path from impulse to saved entry.

Three taps

Open, pick an activity, save. Fill in afterwards or start a timer — both are first-class paths, neither an afterthought.

One link invites

Share the link and your colleague is in. No approval, no invite codes to email around.

Works offline

Entries queue locally and sync when you are back online. No time lost on the train or in the basement.

Widget and timer

Add 30 or 60 minutes straight from the home screen. A running timer shows on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Your country's calendar

Public holidays for your country — and for German states and the UK's nations — so a day off never shows up as missing time.

Privacy that is built in, not promised

The group decides how much is shared — and the rules are enforced by the database, not by the interface.

Open

Every member sees everyone's entries and names. Good for small teams who sit together anyway.

Summarised

Totals per activity, without names. You see where the time goes — but nobody sees who did what.

Private

Everyone only sees their own. The owner can export aggregates for follow-up.

Nobody can edit anyone else's time — not even the owner. That is a deliberate rule, not a gap.

Pricing

The owner pays for the whole group. No member ever meets a paywall.
